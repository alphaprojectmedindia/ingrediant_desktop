        Label detective tips

        • The first three ingredients are most of the product.
        • Sugar hides under many names: syrup, glucose, dextrose, maltodextrin.
        • A number in brackets (like 211) is an additive's INS / E-number.
        • "Fruit drink" is not the same as "fruit juice".
        • Shorter lists usually mean less processing.
